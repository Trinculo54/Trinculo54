Questions

Wait, wasn't FH2MP free?expand_more

It was. Then we looked at World of Warcraft and thought "they seem fine."

Do I still need my own copy of the game?expand_more

Yes. Your own copy of the Xbox One build 1.0.0.64, a PC on par with a Steam Deck, an Xbox controller, and now a credit card.

Does paying give me a gameplay advantage?expand_more

No. Except that you can play the game at all, which some would call an advantage.

What happens if my subscription lapses?expand_more

Your cars are kept safe for 7 years. Your Car Club will kick you after 3 days.

When is Storm Island coming online?expand_more

It will be the next expansion: FH2MP: Wrath of the Storm Island. Pre-orders open soon. Pre-ordering does not make it come out sooner.

Keep it running

FH2MP is free $14.99/month. Support pays for the servers and gets you no gameplay advantage (except all of it).
